Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Kumalo ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information is collected when you visit, and how it is used.

What we collect

We don't require accounts and we don't ask for personal information to read the site. Information handled here is limited to:

  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, we store the email address you provide and use it only to send the newsletter. Unsubscribe any time via the link in each email.
  • Contact: if you email us, we keep the correspondence to respond to you.
  • Log data: our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance.

Advertising and cookies

This site displays advertising, which may include Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Directly sold ads on this site are labelled "Sponsored" or "Advertisement" and do not set cookies of their own.

Third-party links

Articles link to external sites (video platforms, ticket sellers, social media). Their privacy practices are their own; this policy covers only this site.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, browse with personalised ads disabled, and request deletion of any data you've shared with us (such as a newsletter email) by contacting us.
